Use the supplied prices and included services literally. Do not raise Premium prices to exceed Standard or add customary detailing treatments to a package.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires every supplied service and price without omissions, price changes or invented additions. The supplied exterior Premium price for extra-large vehicles is intentionally preserved below Standard, despite the unusual ordering.

**How to apply:** For catalog revisions, verify the complete supplied list in all three languages, including price variants, extra-service notices, explanatory pages and email estimates. Do not treat a non-monotonic price as a typo without asking.

Vehicle pricing has three groups: compact cars and coupés, saloons and SUVs, and extra-large vehicles including 7-seaters, vans and delivery vans. SUVs belong in the middle group, not a fourth category.

**Why:** The user replaced the former four-category pricing with this explicit three-category classification.

**How to apply:** Keep forms, price displays, explanatory copy and messages consistent with these groups.

Quote requests are open. Do not restore the prelaunch notice or submission hold.

**Why:** On 2026-10-20 the user explicitly requested removing the not-yet-operating messaging across the site and making the forms work, superseding the earlier prelaunch hold.

**How to apply:** Preserve validation, request delivery and success states when changing forms. Only close requests again if the user explicitly asks.
